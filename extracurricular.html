Coffee Rankings

I spend more time (and money) at coffee shops than I would like to admit. Below is a running ranking of my favorite coffee shops in a few cities I have spent a non-trivial amount of time living in.1

Pittsburgh
# Name Notes
1 KLVN Coffee Lab I found this place by accident on Google Maps and have had a hard time making an excuse to frequent other coffee shops since. I recommend trying the Mescalero (if you like Mezcal).
2 Mechanic Coffee I would say they have the best coffee in Pittsburgh.
3 Creative Chem Co Newer establishment with an incredibly creative interior design and friendly staff.
4 Shibam Coffee Best hours of all coffee shops in Pittsburgh (7am - 11pm everyday).
5 Commonplace Coffee Favorite place to go to buy coffee beans for drip coffee.
Paris
# Name Notes
1 Terres de Cafe A chain with several locations, still the highest quality coffee I've had.
2 Noir Even with 18 locations the quality is consistently great. I wish the seating was as functional as it is aesthetic though.
3 Dose Really nice spot to go hang out at for Sunday brunch.
4 Araku Most of my visits were on coffee breaks from the adjacent BSG library. Great coffee.
5 Aura Feels like you're working out of a café in Newport Beach. Really good drink and food options.

I have to say that I spent too much time in the BSG library to get as much out of Parisian coffee shops as I would have liked. Going to coffee shops there on the weekends is also a bit of a gamble as many places don’t allow laptops.

Side note, I found this incredible website on all things Parisian coffee that I would have loved to have known about when I lived there.

New York
# Name Notes
1 Voyager Espresso Coolest owners and great coffee.
2 Ninth street espresso This is genuinely the only place I have logged where I have had genuinely bad experiences with the service. But the coffee and location are top notch.
3 Thayer Favorite place to work out of in East Village. I recommend the honey-nut cheerio latte.
4 Blue Bottle Great location facing the NYSE.
5 Black Fox While very pricey, the quality is undeniable.
6 Suited Nice location (holding the fact that it is in FiDi fixed).
7 Elsewhere Espresso. Cozy and underrated weekend spot.
